# 2. The biggest problem I see

The website appears to be competing in a **high-trust, high-intent industry**:

> Overseas education + work visas + immigration/visa consulting.

That's a difficult SEO category.

People aren't searching only for:

> “Siddhivinayak Overseas”

They search for things like:

* study visa consultant
* study abroad consultant
* work visa consultant
* overseas education consultant
* Canada study visa consultant
* UK study visa consultant
* Australia study visa consultant
* Germany study visa consultant
* overseas job consultant
* work permit consultant
* visa consultant near me
* best study visa consultant in [city]
* [country] work visa consultant in [city]

The site therefore needs to become an **information + service authority**, not just a company brochure.

---

# 3. There's another major issue: brand ambiguity

Search results show several different businesses using very similar **Siddhivinayak Overseas** names.

For example, search results surface businesses associated with:

* Jasdan/Rajkot and agricultural exports ([Justdial][2])
* Surat and spices/export activity ([Volza][3])
* Anand and tourism/travel services ([Justdial][4])
* Delhi and visa assistance ([Justdial][5])

This is **very important for SEO and branding**.

Google needs extremely clear signals about:

**Who are you?
Where are you located?
What exactly do you do?
Which countries/services do you specialize in?
Which business listings belong to you?**

Otherwise your brand authority gets diluted.
